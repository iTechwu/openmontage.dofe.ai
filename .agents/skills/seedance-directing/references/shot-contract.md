# Director's Read And Shot Contract

## Narrative Record

Complete these fields internally before drafting a narrative shot:

1. `dramatic_function`: introduce, deepen, test, reveal, decide, or pay off.
2. `turn`: the visible before-to-after value change.
3. `pov`: whose experience controls what is revealed.
4. `power_shift`: who controls the beat at the start and end.
5. `objective`: what the focal subject tries to get, protect, avoid, or prove.
6. `obstacle_and_tactic`: what blocks it and the playable response.
7. `subtext`: the contradiction between surface behavior and intent.
8. `suppressed_behavior`: one small visible impulse that is contained or leaks.
9. `specific_detail`: one source-bound or deliberately authored detail that a
   generic version of this story could not reuse unchanged.
10. `stock_solution_refused`: the easiest genre shortcut and the concrete
    replacement used instead.

Do not copy these labels into the final generation prompt. Translate them into
blocking, action, eyeline, prop use, distance, light, dialogue contradiction,
silence, sound, and a visible endpoint.

## Utility Record

For product, material, camera-transfer, ambient, process, or VFX shots without
agency, record only `utility_intent` and `non_narrative_refusal`. Do not invent
psychology or conflict.

## Coherence Check

For each craft choice, finish the sentence: `This choice exists because the
shot must...`. Replace any choice that cannot be tied to the felt intent.

## Allocation Check

Choose one primary spend from identity fidelity, motion boldness, or scene
density. Choose one secondary spend and state what is economized. Move exact
text and logos to post rather than spending generation fidelity on them.
