# Reference Authority

Give every reference exactly one primary role, a `binding_mode`, and both
transfer and non-transfer. Use a provider token only when the selected tool
contract declares its syntax; otherwise bind via the provider input field.

```text
@Image1 controls vehicle identity only; preserve body geometry, paint, lights,
and wheel design. Ignore its background, camera angle, lighting, and text.
```

Canonical identity references control immutable identity. An accepted previous
clip or final frame controls the transient opening state. A donor video may
control motion, camera, or timing only when explicitly authorized. Never let a
motion reference overwrite identity, wardrobe, product geometry, or canon.

When a reference contains several subjects, identify the intended subject by
tag, position, role, or visible feature. Do not assume the largest or central
subject is the target.
